Background

Research Data Management (RDM) has emerged as a cornerstone of contemporary research practice, ensuring data integrity, reproducibility, and compliance with ethical and legal standards. Effective RDM is essential for leveraging the full potential of research data, making it easier to discover, access, and reuse.

Key challenges in RDM include metadata creation, data interoperability, and adherence to FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) principles. Artificial Intelligence (AI) offers transformative solutions to these challenges by automating complex tasks, enhancing metadata quality, and facilitating FAIR compliance through advanced techniques such as machine learning, knowledge graphs, and natural language processing.

AIRDeM 2025 aims to explore the intersection of AI and RDM, bringing together researchers, practitioners, and domain experts to address these challenges and push the boundaries of what is possible in RDM. The workshop provides a platform for knowledge exchange, collaboration, and the development of innovative tools and methodologies.
