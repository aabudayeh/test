About

Overview

In today's data-driven research landscape, effective Research Data Management (RDM) practices are crucial for unlocking the full potential of innovative discoveries while upholding scientific integrity and reproducibility...

Timeliness

RDM is increasingly challenging due to data volume, heterogeneity, and a lack of scalable tools. LLMs and AI methods are revolutionizing data management by enabling automation in key areas...

Workshop Topics

Metadata Automation

Discussing LLMs for metadata enrichment and improving accessibility.

FAIR Metrics

Developing metrics to measure and improve FAIR compliance.

Metadata Quality

Using knowledge graphs to enhance metadata quality and searchability.

AI Question Answering for RDM

AI-driven tools for navigation, summarization, and exploration.

Data Quality and Harmonization

AI methods for handling missing, inconsistent, and heterogeneous data.

Goals

We invite attendees to contribute to the AIRDeM workshop at ESWC 2025 by submitting research papers, demos, and posters that employ AI to advance RDM practices...
