About

In today's data-driven research landscape, effective Research Data Management (RDM) practices are crucial for unlocking the full potential of innovative discoveries, while upholding the integrity and reproducibility of scientific work. FAIR (Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability) principles help ensure that the data across applications remains discoverable, accessible and usable. The Artificial Intelligence for Research Data Management (AIRDeM) workshop explores the application of AI-driven solutions and interdisciplinary approaches for RDM, focussing on topics like automated metadata extraction, annotation, and generation. The workshop will explore how these AI techniques can help in improving data findability, accessibility, and quality, interpretability and searchability. Practically, we invite papers covering methods using AI for enhancing, developing and measuring FAIR metrics to improve data discovery and interoperability. This may include AI-assisted metadata generation pipelines, the use of Knowledge Graphs (KGs) to enrich metadata and increase findability, as well as incorporation of AI driven questions answering to facilitate data exploration. AIRDeM aims at gathering research papers and practical implementations (demos) across multiple domains, including life sciences, earth sciences, and digital humanities. In addition, the workshop will feature a collaborative shared task entitled "Bring Your Challenge." This initiative invites participants to present real-world RDM problems, such as metadata creation, FAIR compliance, or data interoperability, and provides a platform for the community to collaboratively explore AI-driven solutions. By fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, this shared task aims to generate innovative approaches to address pressing RDM challenges. By integrating Large Language models (LLMs), information extraction (IE), and neuro-symbolic AI with RDM practices, AIRDeM aims to foster an interdisciplinary community committed to addressing RDM challenges and advancing FAIR practices through cutting-edge AI applications.
